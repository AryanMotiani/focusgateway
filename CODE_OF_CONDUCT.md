# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Regimen to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, background, experience level, identity or where they come from. Many contributors are students and first-time open source contributors. Treat every question as a good question.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give feedback on the work, never on the person.
- Accept that people disagree, and assume good intent.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or repeatedly derailing discussions.
- Any behaviour that would be inappropriate in a classroom or workplace.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact in the repository profile, or with GitHub's "Report content" option. All reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, depending on how serious and repeated it is.
