Install the Regimen lock agent

The lock agent makes your Regimen blocks work in every browser and app on this Mac, Safari included.

  • It runs quietly in the background and starts with your Mac.
  • It turns off Secure DNS and private windows in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox, so blocks can't be skipped.
  • It never connects to the internet. Your data stays on this Mac.

After installing, your browser opens Regimen and connects the agent by itself.
